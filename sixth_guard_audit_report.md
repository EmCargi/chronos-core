# Sixth Guard Leak Audit

A greeting LEAKS only if it asserts the character is IMMUNE to their Sixth Guard, that the guard CANNOT/WILL NOT ever trigger, or that the collapse is PERMANENTLY and unconditionally bypassed/cured.

These are NOT leaks (by engine design): the player arriving and softening the blow (meet-hook); a character planning/preparing for or embracing their guard; a one-time emergency measure used as a contingency; survival via ordinary competence or player help.

Leaks found: 0
